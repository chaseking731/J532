How Navient's transition to Aidvantage increased student loan complaints

Navient gave away their student loans in December 2021. Aidvantage took over 5.6 million Department of Education-owned student loan accounts according to Navient. In the first year under Aidvantage, the complaints regarding student loans have increased by 41.7%.

The first year of transition was problematic for many former-Navient borrowers. The complaints consisted of misapplied payments, long customer service delays and problems resolving account discrepancies. In the following years with Aidvantage the number of complaints regarding student loans consisting "navient" would lower to 998 in 2023, but would raise to 2,820 in 2024.
